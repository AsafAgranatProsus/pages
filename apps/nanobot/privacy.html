Nanobot — Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Nanobot is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Asaf Agranat. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner’s permission, Nanobot reads the owner’s own Google account data — Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive — to help the owner with their own tasks (for example, summarising email or finding a document). It can create documents in the owner’s Drive when asked.

What it does not do

  • It does not access anyone else’s account.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising.

Nanobot’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and removal

Access tokens are stored on the owner’s private server. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
